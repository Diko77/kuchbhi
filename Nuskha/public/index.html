Clinical Governance

Medical Safety, Clinical Governance & Privacy Policy

Ethical AI principles, bioequivalence caveats, and strict data confidentiality

1. Informational Comparison Only (No Prescribing Advice)

Nuskha AI is developed strictly as a decision-support, screening, and educational tool for a hackathon. It is NOT an automated prescribing engine and does NOT give clinical advice to initiate, alter, or discontinue prescription medicines.

2. Understanding Bioequivalence and Generic Substitution

An identical active molecule does not automatically mean that two products are interchangeable for an individual patient:

  • Release Kinetics: Immediate-release formulations (e.g. Metformin IR) and Extended-release formulations (e.g. Glucophage XR) have entirely different pharmacokinetic profiles. Substituting between them without medical dosage adjustments can cause adverse effects.
  • Narrow Therapeutic Index (NTI): Critical medications like Warfarin (Coumadin) have a narrow margin between safety and toxicity. Even approved generic substitutions require laboratory INR monitoring.
  • Inactive Excipients: Binders, dyes, and coatings can differ across generic manufacturers, posing potential allergy risks.

3. Honest Price Policy

Real-world pharmaceutical prices vary drastically depending on regional healthcare systems (e.g. NHS, Medicare, private co-pays, and manufacturer rebates). Nuskha AI never fabricates or estimates commercial pricing.

4. Strict Patient Privacy

Prescription images and health data are processed strictly in client-side volatile memory. No images or patient details are persisted or uploaded to external tracking servers.
